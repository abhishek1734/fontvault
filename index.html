ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why I built FontVault

I built FontVault as a personal project because I got tired of having fifteen tabs open just to find and compare fonts. Flipping back and forth between Google Fonts, Fontshare, and Adobe Fonts to test whether two typefaces looked good together was frustrating.

FontVault puts quality free and open-source fonts into one clean interface with live UI mockups, side-by-side comparison, an AI pairing helper, and direct downloads. No paywalls, no corporate licensing tiers, no signup required — just a useful tool for anyone who cares about type.
